    ROVERVR DRIVE

    Full-screen voice driving with live camera, danger glows, speed levels and a path map.

    1. Connect the car (Bluetooth or USB) and the phone camera on the main page first.
    2. Lift the wheels for the first test. Keep this tab in front.
    3. Say “forward”, “turn right 30 degrees and move forward”, “speed level 3”, “analyze”, “stop”.
    4. Missions: “record route” → drive → “stop recording”, then “repeat route” · “go home” · “go to the bottle” · “follow me” · “what do you see?” · “stop following”.
    5. Autopilot: “autopilot 200 cm”. Correct it any time with “left 20” / “dodge right”; “hold” pauses, “resume” continues, “cancel autopilot” gives you control.

    Keys: W/↑ forward · S/↓ reverse · A/D turn 15° · SPACE hold · +/− speed · R analyze · M mic · Esc E-stop

    Danger glows are warnings only. The car stops by itself if something is 12 cm or closer ahead, if this page closes, or if the connection drops. Distances, angles, speed and the path are estimates.
